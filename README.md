# 📊 Online Sales Data Analysis using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes an online sales dataset using SQL to extract meaningful business insights. The goal is to identify sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
- Dataset Name: Online Sales Data  
- Format: CSV  
- Type: Transaction-level sales data  

### Key Columns
- order_date  
- product_category  
- product_name  
- region  
- units_sold  
- revenue  
- payment_method  
- customer_id  

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
1. What are the distinct product categories?
2. Which product category is the most selling?
3. What is the most selling product?
4. Which region generates the highest revenue?
5. Who are the top 10 repeat customers?
6. Which date and month record the highest sales?
7. What is the total revenue by product category?
8. What is the most used payment method?
9. What are the total units sold and total revenue?
10. Which month is most suitable for offers and discounts?
11. Which category is least and most important?

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- SQL (MySQL)
- Aggregate Functions
- GROUP BY & ORDER BY
- Date Functions

---

## ✅ Conclusion
This project demonstrates the practical use of SQL to answer real-world business questions and generate actionable insights for business decision-making.
